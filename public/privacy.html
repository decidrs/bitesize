Privacy

Morsel is published by Strateriq Ltd, registered in England and Wales, company number 15913960. Morsel does not collect, store or share personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

On your device. The app remembers which cards you have seen and which you have saved, so it can show you new cards first. This list stays on your phone or in your browser. We never receive it. Deleting the app or clearing your browser data removes it.

Daily reminder (iPhone app only). If you turn on the daily reminder, your phone schedules it locally. We do not send it and cannot see whether it is on.

Sharing. When you share a card, your phone's own share sheet handles it. We do not see what you share or who with.

Hosting. The website is served by our hosting provider, which, like any web server, processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect the service. We do not use that data to identify you.

Emails to us. If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Contact. hello@strateriq.com

Changes. If this ever changes, we will update this page before the change takes effect.
