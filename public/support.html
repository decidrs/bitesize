Support

Morsel shows you one short, interesting fact per screen. Swipe up for the next one. Each day, Today's 5 gives you five new cards.

Contact us: hello@strateriq.com. We read every message and reply as soon as we can.

A card looks wrong

Cards are written with AI assistance and checked, but mistakes can slip through. Tap the flag on the card, or email us with the card's title, and we will look at it.

How does the streak work?

Finish Today's 5 to keep your streak going. If you miss a day, one free pass each week (Monday to Sunday) keeps it alive.

Turning the daily reminder on or off (iPhone)

Tap Daily in the top bar. You can also turn notifications off for Morsel in your iPhone's Settings.

Where are my saved cards?

Tap Saved in the top bar. Saved cards and your streak are kept on your device only, so they do not move to a new phone and are removed if you delete the app.
